At 2,400 feet above sea level, dawn over the Chimbuk range arrives without ceremony. First comes the sapphire chill of early mist drifting along the valleys, then the sudden, golden slash of sun crowning the peaks of Bandarban.

Trekking Nilgiri during the transition into late autumn demands an appreciation for fleeting atmospheres. The indigenous Bawm hamlets perched along the ridgelines begin their morning fires, sending sweet birch smoke twisting up into the low-hanging cloud formations. Unlike the well-trodden river circuits of Sylhet or the coastal sands of Inani, these highland ridges offer quiet, contemplative vastness.

"The clouds do not pass Nilgiri; they rest here, washing the serpentine hill-tract roads in cool silver condensation."

In this field guide, we chart the three-day traverse from Ruma Bazaar through the remote waterfalls of Rijuk, culminating at the southern viewpoints where cloud seas linger well into midday.
